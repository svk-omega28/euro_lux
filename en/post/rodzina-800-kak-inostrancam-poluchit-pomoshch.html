Rodzina 800+ — How Foreigners Get Help

What is Rodzina 800+ in Poland? This initiative is aimed at supporting demographic development in Poland, giving families the opportunity to receive monthly financial assistance in the amount of PLN 800 for each child.


From January 1, 2024, the amount of the childcare benefit will be increased from PLN 500 to PLN 800. ZUS, which pays this benefit, will change its amount itself. Therefore, you do not need to submit an additional application. Benefit payments in the new amount will be made from January 2024.


On August 7, 2023, the President of the Republic of Poland, Andrzej Duda, signed an amendment to the Act on state aid in raising children. This means that from January 1, 2024, the amount of the parental benefit (so-called 500+) will be PLN 800 for each child until they turn 18.



From January 1, 2024, the amount of the childcare benefit for all eligible persons will be increased from PLN 500 to PLN 800. For parents and guardians, this means that more funds will be transferred to their accounts each year to support the child. In the case of one child, it will be PLN 3,600 more, for two children - PLN 7,200, and for three children - PLN 10,800.


The program covers not only Polish citizens, but also certain groups of foreign residents living in the country (more details on the criteria for non-Polish citizens eligible for the Rodzina 800+ program will be provided below).


Why is the Rodzina 800+ Program Relevant?


The relevance of the "Rodzina 800+" program is directly related to the acute problem of demographic decline in Poland, a phenomenon, unfortunately, that is far from new. Although a detailed analysis of the causes and consequences of this phenomenon is beyond the scope of our discussion, it is important to highlight some key data to understand the scale of the situation.


As an illustration, we will cite the period from 2010 to 2015, during which a steady decline in the country's population was observed. Thus, if in 2010 the demographic increase was approximately 33,000 people, then by 2015 a decrease of 41,500 people was recorded, which emphasizes the trend towards a continuing demographic decline.


Who Can Get 800 PLN Per Child?


We will define the categories of persons who have the right to participate in the program. This includes all Polish citizens, as well as certain foreign nationals (listed below) who have children. According to the changes adopted in 2019, the level of family income is no longer a criterion for receiving assistance; the subsidy is provided to all families, starting with the first child, without exception.


Only those who actually live in Poland have the right to receive this subsidy. Citizens of Poland and foreigners living or working abroad, even in another member state of the European Union, are not provided with this financial assistance.


The government has the power to verify an applicant's residency in the country using a variety of methods, including physical verification of a person's residence and analysis of European tax databases.


Can Foreigners Get Support?


The issue of the availability of the "Rodzina 800+" program for foreigners deserves special attention. Not all foreign nationals can claim this subsidy, although it is available to many.
Foreign nationals who:



  • They have a residence permit in connection with the performance of qualified work.

  • They have a residence permit that entitles them to work.


The legislation does not require a permanent residence permit in Poland to receive benefits, but there are restrictions for foreigners from countries outside the EU. Yes, foreigners who work under a visa, have a residence permit for less than six months or are employed in seasonal work cannot receive a subsidy. This means that most visa subsidy options are limited.


The need to have a PESEL identification number also emphasizes the requirement for foreigner status; in cases where the PESEL is mandatory and cannot be replaced by the NIP number, the foreigner's residence status must meet the established requirements.


In 2022, the right to receive a subsidy was extended to include refugees from Ukraine who have obtained UKR status. The details of the process of registration of the "800+" assistance for Ukrainian refugees will be considered separately.


Instructions for Issuing a Subsidy Under the "Rodzina 800+" Program


Since 2022, the submission of applications for public services, including the assistance program, in Poland has moved to a completely online format. Several channels are provided for this: Internet banking, the official website of the Insurance Institution of the Collateral (ZUS), which is now responsible for processing applications, and the Emp@tia platform, which we will discuss in more detail. The application process through the Emp@tia platform includes the following stages:


Step 1: Preparation of documents


It is necessary to collect a standard set of documents, which includes:



  • Foreign passport with a visa (if available) or a document confirming the right to residence and access to the labor market.

  • Documents confirming marital status.

  • Children's birth certificates.

  • Completed application.


It should be noted that all documents not in Polish (except the passport) must be officially translated with an apostille, if required through a sworn translator.


ZUS reserves the right to request additional documents, although such cases are rare.


This transition to digital submission of applications simplifies the process of applying for state aid, making it more accessible and convenient for all categories of the population, including foreign citizens, who are children under the age of 18. Amendments to the law are being discussed, which will allow continuation of payments for adult students, but at the time of the current discussion, these changes have not yet been adopted in Poland.


Step 2: Application procedure


The ZUS is responsible for the consideration of applications. In certain situations, applications can be sent directly to social support bodies or other state and municipal institutions.


Applications are submitted exclusively online, requiring applicants to pass identification through systems such as Internet banking, PUE ZUS or ePUAP profile. The application process through Emp@tia will be discussed separately.


Detailed instructions for submitting an application online:



  • Start by visiting the Emp@tia portal.

  • Complete the registration process using one of the available methods:
    1. Internet banking data of leading Polish banks (eg PKO, Inteligo, Pekao).
    2. Zaufany profile.
    3. Electronic signature.

  • Choose the appropriate method of identification, enter the required data.

  • Upload copies of documents as needed.

  • Submit your application for review.


This process emphasizes the digitization of citizens' interaction with government services, simplifying access to support programs and ensuring the convenience and efficiency of submitting applications.



After sending the application for participation in the program, a notification will be sent to the indicated contact details about the start of its consideration. This is a critically important stage, because without receiving such confirmation, the process is considered not to have started.


Step 2.2: Alternative application method through PUE ZUS


This method is not a continuation of the previous one, but is another option for submitting an application, proposed in step 2.1. To implement it, you need to register on the PUE ZUS platform.
Instructions for using this method include the following steps:



  1. Visit the official website of ZUS.

  2. Go through the authorization procedure.

  3. In the menu, select the sections "Ogólny", "Ubezpieczony" or "Świadczeniobiorca".

  4. On the left side of the menu, find and click on the "Rodzina 800+" option.

  5. Click on "Utwórz nowy wniosek".


Thus, it is possible to choose a convenient way to apply for participation in the program, ensuring accessibility and flexibility of the process for all potential participants.




After filling out, you need to submit an application. Information about the decision on the granting of a benefit can be found in the section "Świadczeniobiorca" → "Dokumenty i szączy" → "Skrzynka dłobicza".


Step 3: Terms of consideration of the application


There are no specific deadlines for considering applications, but there is a requirement to make the first payment by the end of the month following the month of submitting the application.


For example, if the application is submitted on January 25, the first payment must be made by February 28 (29), which means that by this time the decision on the application will have already been made. This applies to the initial submission; in case of re-submission, provided the documents are submitted on time and there are no changes to the grounds, payments will be made regularly.


Step 4: Receiving payments


If the application is approved, the funds will be transferred to the bank account specified in the registration process. From 2022, this is the only way to receive assistance; receiving cash through the cash desk or postal transfers is no longer provided.



Frequently Asked Questions About the Rodzina 800+ Program


After getting acquainted with the key information about the "Rodzina 800+" program, we will consider the answers to the most frequently asked questions about its terms and procedures.


When to apply?


Applications for assistance are submitted every year. It is important that:



  • Payments under the program are made from June to May of the following year.

  • In order for the aid to be paid without interruption, the application must be submitted by the end of April each year.

  • From 2022, applications are accepted exclusively online, starting on February 1.

  • Submission of applications is also possible after May 1, but in this case, payments will begin from the month of application submission.


What is the amount of assistance?


The amount of assistance is PLN 800 per month for each child eligible for a subsidy under the "Rodzina 800+" program.


What is the duration of the assistance?


Assistance is provided to children until they reach 18 years of age. Amendments to the law are being discussed to allow continuation of payments for adult students, but at the time of the current discussion these changes have not yet been adopted.
