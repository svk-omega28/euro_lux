Increase in the Minimum Wage in Poland from July 1, 2024

A new increase in the minimum wage will come into effect in Poland on July 1, 2024, which will have a significant impact on many families and workers across the country. The minimum wage will be increased to PLN 4,300 gross per month, which is PLN 58 more than the previous level established from January 1, 2024.


What does this increase mean?



  1. Increase in Minimum Wage:



  • From January 1, 2024, the minimum wage was PLN 4,242 gross.

  • From July 1, 2024, the minimum wage will be PLN 4,300 gross.



  1. Increase in Hourly Rate:



  • From 1 January 2024, the minimum hourly rate was PLN 27.70.

  • From July 1, 2024, the minimum hourly rate will be increased to PLN 28.10.


Impact on Workers and Employers


This minimum wage increase is aimed at improving the standard of living for those earning the lowest wages, as well as combating inflation. Raising the minimum wage provides workers with additional financial stability and increases their purchasing power.


For employers, this increase also means an increase in labor costs. The Polish government estimates that in 2024 the total cost to employers due to the minimum wage increase will be around PLN 35.34 billion.



Why is it important?


The Polish government has been systematically raising the minimum wage over recent years to reduce the income gap and improve working conditions. Since the beginning of 2015, the country's minimum wage has more than doubled, a significant step towards social justice and an improved quality of life for many workers.


Conclusion


The increase in the minimum wage from 1 July 2024 is an important development for Polish workers and employers. It reflects the government's commitment to improving working conditions and living standards, and is part of a broader strategy to combat economic inequalities.
